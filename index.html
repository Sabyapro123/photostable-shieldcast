The prescription that reads the sky

A Photostable prescription that knows today's UV, times every reapplication and finds the next tube.

Dr Iyer writes a Shield Plan. Its QR code opens a daily plan built from the live UV forecast for the patient's city. There is no app to install, no login and no health data on a server, because the prescription lives inside the link.

    Course RxA dose and a course, like any other prescription

    The doctor writes 12 weeks, and the plan turns that into a number of tubes. The patient knows on day one that one tube is not the whole treatment.

    Season SwitchThe texture changes with the weather, at the refill

    Gold for humid spells and Emulgel for dry ones, only if the doctor allows it. One patient uses two Photostable variants across the year and never holds two tubes in a day.

    SunDotA cap that shows when UV is up

    A UV-reactive dot on the cap turns violet when the UV index passes 3. It works without a phone. In a field trial, UV stickers lifted reapplication from 68% to 80% (Hacker et al., 2019).

    30% to 56%

    Daily sunscreen use rose from 30% to 56% when people got a text with the local weather and a reminder (Armstrong et al., Archives of Dermatology, 2009, randomised, 70 adults).

    35%

    Only 35% of Indian melasma patients used any sunscreen in a nine-centre study of 331 patients (KrupaShankar et al., Dermatology and Therapy, 2014).

    15%

    Visible-light protection improved melasma scores by 15% more than UV-only sunscreen in a double-blind trial (Castanedo-Cazares et al., 2014). This is why Pro+ stays every day for melasma.

    The same forecast that times Ananya's reapplication tells Sun where to move stock

    Tubes run out faster in weeks that need more reapplication. ShieldCast reads the 7-day UV and humidity outlook for each metro and flags where partner pharmacies should be topped up before demand arrives.

    Loading the outlookUV load is the sum of each day's peak UV above 5 over the next 7 days.
    This is a hypothesis to test, not a proven rule. The pilot will compare stockist sell-out with the UV load in each city for 28 weeks and fit the real relationship before Sun changes any stock norms.
    ShieldCast Agent Desk

    Four agents run the loop, and a person approves every step that matters

    How it works

    Everything connects through one signed link and one public forecast

    The doctor's choices are written into the QR link itself. The patient's phone reads the link and the live forecast and works out the day's plan on the device. Nothing about the patient is stored on a server unless she agrees to reminders.
